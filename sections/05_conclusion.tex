\section{Conclusion}
\newrev{This paper presents an MBA-RL framework for coordinated charging and SOC balancing of lithium-ion battery packs. Each agent regulates the charging current of one cell, while a QMIX mixing network coordinates the pack-level objective and a model-based screen checks the requested currents before execution. The framework therefore operates without a dedicated equalization topology. Over 60 evaluation runs covering 12 unseen initial-SOC conditions and five independent training runs, MBA-RL reduces accumulated SOC imbalance by 12.65\% and 10.82\% relative to shared IQL and centralized DQN under the same discrete action setting. The safety-constrained mechanism satisfies the sampled limits in all 52 safety tests and enables all tests to reach the charging objective when included during training. These results support coordinated cell-level charging and pack-level balancing under the examined model-based conditions. Future work will validate the framework in hardware-in-the-loop and larger-scale battery systems.}
