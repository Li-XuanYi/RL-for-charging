\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents an MBA-RL framework for coordinated charging and SOC balancing of lithium-ion battery packs. Each agent regulates the charging current of one cell, while QMIX coordinates the local charging decisions through a shared battery-pack objective. A model-based safety mechanism checks the requested currents before execution, allowing the framework to perform SOC balancing without a dedicated equalization topology.
Across 60 evaluations covering 12 unseen initial SOC conditions and five independent training runs, MBA-RL reduces accumulated SOC imbalance by 12.65\% and 10.82\% relative to shared IQL and centralized DQN, respectively, under the same discrete current setting. The safety mechanism satisfies the evaluated operating limits in all 52 safety tests. When the same mechanism is included during training, all 52 tests also reach the charging objective.
Future work will evaluate MBA-RL in hardware-in-the-loop experiments and larger battery packs with more realistic electrical, power, and thermal interactions.
