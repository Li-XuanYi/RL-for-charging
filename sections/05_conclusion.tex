\section{Conclusion}

\newrev{This paper presented MBA-RL for coordinating independently regulated cell charging currents toward fast charging and pack-level SOC balance. The framework uses QMIX for cooperative value learning and a model-based mechanism to screen current commands against predicted operating limits. Including this mechanism during training allows the agents to learn the effects of current interventions, while nonnegative current commands avoid deliberate discharge for balancing.}

\newrev{The simulation studies show that execution-time screening and training with the screening mechanism have distinct effects on constraint satisfaction and task completion. Comparisons and ablations support the role of the balancing reward and parameter sharing in the tested setting, but do not establish a general performance advantage for QMIX or a consistent benefit from all of its components. These findings support further investigation of coordinated charging in systems with independently controllable cell currents.}

\newrev{The present evidence is limited by discharge-based model calibration, sensitivity to observation errors and thermal conditions, and incomplete validation of larger coupled packs. It does not establish charging-domain model accuracy or physical closed-loop performance. Future work will validate the controller with independent charging measurements and hardware-in-the-loop tests, improve state estimation and uncertainty handling, and extend the evaluation to packs with shared electrical, power, and thermal constraints.}
