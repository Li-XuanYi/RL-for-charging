\section{Conclusion}
\newrev{This paper presents MBA-RL for coordinated cell charging and SOC balancing without a dedicated equalization topology. A QMIX controller learns coordinated policies through centralized training, while each agent uses local observations during execution. A model-based safety mechanism checks the requested currents before they are applied. In simulation tests with the identified cell models, MBA-RL reduced accumulated SOC imbalance by 12.65\% and 10.82\% relative to shared IQL and centralized DQN under the same discrete current set. The safety mechanism satisfied the constraints at sampled times in all 52 safety tests; incorporating it during training also enabled all tests to complete charging. Performance remained sensitive to observation errors, high ambient temperature, and increasing pack size. Future work will examine hardware-in-the-loop operation and larger battery systems with electrical and thermal coupling.}
